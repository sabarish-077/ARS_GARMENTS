Frequently asked questions

Find a little more about ARS Garments and our everyday essentials.

What kind of products does ARS Garments make?

We make comfortable innerwear and everyday knitwear, including trunks, vests, briefs, camisoles and casual tees.

Where are ARS Garments products made?

Our home is Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu, India, a city known for its textile and knitwear industry.

Yes. Our B2B team can discuss production runs, job work and private label requirements.
